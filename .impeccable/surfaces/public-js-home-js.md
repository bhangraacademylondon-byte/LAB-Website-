# Home page

Scope: the public home page (/). Visitor mode: Persuade.

Audience: parents of children and adults who want to dance, equally; mostly on phones, often arriving from Instagram. Action: come along on a Thursday (no booking), so the visitor must leave knowing when, where and how much. Proof: the academy's own Instagram photos and video; teachers' names; no invented reviews or numbers.

## Direction contract

THESIS: the home page is the academy's name set huge beside a wall of rounded tiles that never stops moving: real Instagram photos, navy and gold blocks, and the class's facts one per tile (the day, the time, the price, the area), so the visitor reads when, where and how much while the wall carries the energy. Refuses the dancer-photo hero with a centred slogan over three feature cards.

OWN-WORLD: warm paper ground, a floating white header card, Archivo 900 uppercase tracked tight with Inter for reading and Inter italic for the punchline, rounded 22px tiles in navy, raised navy, midnight, gold and cream with a fine grain, the last word of the name on its own navy tile in gold with a thread of gold shine round it. Flat, no tile shadows. Motion in the spirit of Magic UI, built in plain CSS and JS (shimmer, shine border, word rotate, ripple), all off under reduced motion, with a pause button for the wall.

STORY: the header slides down, the name rises line by line and the tiles glide in and settle into a slow drift; the visitor reads the punchline and the lede (Thursday, the time, the school, the price), sees the facts pass on the tiles and the word tile cycle through who the class is for, then taps Get directions (or Call us on a phone) or follows the Timetable link.

FIRST VIEWPORT: the whole page is one screen and never scrolls, in five layouts chosen by the screen's shape. Desktop and landscape tablet: a 1280 x 800 canvas scaled to fill, the name, punchline, lede and actions at left, three drifting tile columns at right (up to three more on very wide screens), pause button at the wall's bottom right. Portrait tablet: header, the name block, then a band of tiles. Phone: the name block first, the tiles fill the rest, Get directions plus Call us. Landscape phone: the name at left, the tiles in a column at right.

FORM: editorial mosaic, chosen by the user from a supplied reference prompt; seed: none (unavailable)

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
